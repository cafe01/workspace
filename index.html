Install and prepare

Download and inspect the installer, then run sh install-workspace.sh on macOS or Ubuntu, or powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install-workspace.ps1 on Windows. It verifies the complete archive before activation and never asks for administrator access.

Placement is not readiness. Run workspace provision, then workspace provision --check. The application stores its host and prepared runtime in your user-owned application-data location; your folders and source files stay where they are.

Unsigned-preview recovery

Your operating system may warn before opening an unsigned preview. Keep normal system protections enabled. Verify the installer’s source and the release catalog, use the ordinary per-app “Open anyway” or “Run anyway” control only after that check, and contact support if an expected release is absent or verification fails.

For a failed install, rerun the downloaded installer after checking your network and disk space. It preserves the previously active host; do not delete Workspace folders or your workspaces to recover.
